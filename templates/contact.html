Frequently Asked Questions

When our AI detects you're at risk of running out of charge with no nearby stations, it automatically dispatches a mobile charging van to your location. The van provides enough charge to get you to the nearest station or your destination.

We currently cover major metropolitan areas across India including Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Chennai. We're expanding to 15 more cities by the end of 2025.

Our AI-powered range estimation is 94% accurate, factoring in real-time traffic, weather, elevation, driving patterns, and battery health. This accuracy improves as the system learns your specific driving habits.

Subscription plans include monitoring and prediction features. Mobile van service is pay-per-use with discounted rates for subscribers. Charges are based on distance traveled and charge provided, with transparent pricing shown before confirmation.
